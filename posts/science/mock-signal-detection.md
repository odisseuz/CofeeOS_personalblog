## Why it matters

Signal detection theory separates sensitivity from bias, which is useful when you
want to know whether a doctor misses a tumour because the task is hard or because
they are cautious.

| term | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| hit | correctly detecting a signal |
| false alarm | reporting a signal that isn't there |

> The d' is the distance between the two distributions.
